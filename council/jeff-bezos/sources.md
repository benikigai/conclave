# Jeff Bezos — Sources

## Books

- **Invent and Wander: The Collected Writings of Jeff Bezos** — edited by Walter Isaacson (2020). The shareholder letters plus essays and speeches. The primary source for Bezos' thinking in his own words.
- **The Everything Store** — Brad Stone (2013). Comprehensive history of Amazon. Reveals decision-making patterns, management philosophy, and the human cost of relentless growth.
- **Amazon Unbound** — Brad Stone (2021). The sequel, covering the later years. AWS, Alexa, and the transition from founder to… whatever comes next.
- **Working Backwards** — Colin Bryar & Bill Carr (2021). Inside Amazon's practices by former senior leaders. The operational detail behind "Working Backwards" and the six-page memo.

## Interviews

- Lex Fridman Podcast (#405, 2023). Multi-hour conversation on space, AI, decision-making, and mortality.
- Charlie Rose — multiple appearances over the years. Good for tracking the evolution of his thinking.
- Economic Club of Washington, D.C. (2018). Wide-ranging interview. The "regret minimization framework" origin story.
- Axel Springer Award acceptance (2018). Short but revealing on ambition, critics, and long-term thinking.
- re:MARS keynotes — Bezos on AI, robotics, and the future of technology.

## Speeches

- Princeton commencement address (2010). "Cleverness is a gift, kindness is a choice." The most personal public statement on values.
- Amazon all-hands meetings (leaked/reported) — real-time management thinking.
- Blue Origin presentations — Bezos on long-term civilizational thinking and infrastructure.

## Letters

- **Amazon Shareholder Letters (1997-2020)** — The single most important source. Every year, a clear articulation of principles, decisions, and lessons. The 1997 letter ("It's all about the long term") is the foundational document. Available on aboutamazon.com.
- The original 1997 letter is explicitly referenced in every subsequent letter. Read it first.

## Articles

- "Jeff Bezos and the Age of Amazon" — various long-form profiles in The New Yorker, Wired, Bloomberg.
- Amazon's "Leadership Principles" — 16 principles that encode Bezos' management philosophy. Available on Amazon's careers page.

## Other

- Amazon's PR/FAQ documents (examples surfaced in various books) — the "Working Backwards" methodology in practice.
- Blue Origin founding documents and philosophy — Bezos on thinking in centuries, not quarters.
