# Steve Jobs — Sources

## Books

- **Steve Jobs** — Walter Isaacson (2011). Authorized biography. The single most comprehensive source for Jobs' decision-making, values, and contradictions. Read critically — Isaacson's framing isn't always Jobs' framing.
- **Becoming Steve Jobs** — Brent Schlender & Rick Tetzeli (2015). The counterpoint to Isaacson. More sympathetic, more nuanced on the maturation arc. Better on the NeXT and Pixar years.
- **Creative Selection** — Ken Kocienda (2018). Inside Apple's development process. Shows how Jobs' taste operated at the granular level of product decisions.
- **The Presentation Secrets of Steve Jobs** — Carmine Gallo (2009). Useful for voice and rhetorical style analysis.

## Interviews

- All Things Digital (D Conference) — multiple years (2003-2010). Jobs at his sharpest, with Mossberg and Swisher. Long-form, unscripted.
- 1995 interview with Robert Cringely (The Lost Interview). Pre-return-to-Apple. Raw, reflective, less guarded than later appearances.
- Rolling Stone interview with Jeff Goodell (2003). On iTunes, music, and the intersection of technology and art.
- Playboy interview (1985). Young Jobs. Ambitious, arrogant, prophetic.

## Speeches

- Stanford Commencement Address (2005). "Stay hungry, stay foolish." The single most concentrated expression of his philosophy on death, purpose, and intuition.
- iPhone introduction keynote (2007). Masterclass in product storytelling and the reality distortion field.
- Various Apple keynotes (1997-2011). Each one is a lesson in taste, narrative, and the art of making people care.

## Letters & Articles

- Internal Apple emails (various, surfaced through litigation). Unfiltered decision-making in real time.
- Open letter on DRM ("Thoughts on Music," 2007). Strategic thinking in public.

## Other

- Pirates of Silicon Valley (1999 film) — dramatized but useful for the early Apple/NeXT arc.
- Pixar story development sessions — Jobs as creative executive, not just technologist.
