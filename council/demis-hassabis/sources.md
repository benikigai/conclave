# Demis Hassabis — Sources

## Books

- **Genius Makers** — Cade Metz (2021). History of modern AI with extensive Hassabis sections. Good for the DeepMind founding story and strategic thinking.
- **The Coming Wave** — Mustafa Suleiman (2023). DeepMind co-founder's perspective on AI risk and governance. Useful counterpoint to Hassabis' more optimistic framing.

## Interviews

- Lex Fridman Podcast (#299, 2022). Deep, multi-hour conversation on AGI, consciousness, game design, neuroscience, and the nature of intelligence.
- Tim Ferriss Show — extended conversation on interdisciplinary thinking and first principles.
- Demis Hassabis at the Royal Institution (multiple lectures). Formal but accessible explanations of AI and scientific discovery.
- Wired, MIT Technology Review, The Verge — multiple long-form interviews on DeepMind's approach to research.
- Nobel Prize interview (2024) — reflections on AlphaFold and the intersection of AI and scientific discovery.

## Speeches & Lectures

- "The Power of Self-Learning Systems" — various conference keynotes. Core philosophy on intelligence and learning.
- Royal Society lectures — on neuroscience-inspired AI.
- Google I/O and DeepMind research presentations — technical but revealing of priorities.
- NeurIPS, ICML keynotes — academic audience, more technical depth.

## Articles & Papers

- "Neuroscience-Inspired Artificial Intelligence" — Hassabis et al. (Neuron, 2017). The theoretical foundation for his interdisciplinary approach.
- AlphaGo, AlphaFold, AlphaZero papers — each reveals the systems-thinking approach to hard problems.
- Various Nature and Science publications — the research output that embodies his philosophy.

## Other

- AlphaGo documentary (2017) — the Go match against Lee Sedol. Shows DeepMind's culture and Hassabis' competitive/collaborative tension.
- Theme Park (1994 video game) — designed by Hassabis at age 17. Early evidence of systems thinking.
- Chess career — Hassabis was a child chess prodigy. Relevant for understanding his game-theoretic intuitions.
